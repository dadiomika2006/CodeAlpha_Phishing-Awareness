Phishing Awareness Training

What is Phishing?

Phishing is a social‑engineering attack where attackers disguise themselves as trusted entities to trick you into revealing sensitive information.

How to Recognise Phishing Emails

  • Urgent or threatening language
  • Misspelled domain names or URLs
  • Unexpected attachments or links
  • Generic greetings (e.g., "Dear Customer")

Fake Websites – What to Look For

  • HTTPS lock icon missing or mismatched domain
  • Odd URL structure or spelling mistakes
  • Poor visual design or low‑resolution logos
  • Requests for personal data on the landing page

Common Social‑Engineering Tactics

  1. Pre‑texting – creating a fabricated scenario
  2. baiting – offering something attractive (e.g., free‑gift)
  3. Tailgating – following an authorised person into a restricted area
  4. Urgency pressure – “Your account will be closed in 5 minutes!”

Best Practices to Avoid Phishing

  • Never click links or open attachments from unknown senders
  • Verify the sender’s email address carefully
  • Hover over links to view the real URL before clicking
  • Use multi‑factor authentication (MFA) wherever possible
  • Keep software and browsers up‑to‑date
  • Report suspicious emails to your IT/security team

Takeaway

Staying vigilant, verifying sources, and applying security best practices protect you and your organisation from phishing attacks.
